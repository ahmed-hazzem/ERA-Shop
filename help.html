Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Find answers to the most common questions about shopping, shipping, and payments.

How do I create a new account?

You can easily create an account by clicking the "Buy" button on any product details page. You will be automatically redirected to the account registration page.

What payment methods are supported?

We support various payment options including Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard) and Cash on Delivery (COD).

How long will delivery take?

Standard delivery typically takes between 2 to 5 business days depending on your geographical location.

Can I modify my order after placing it?

Yes, you can request order modifications by reaching out through our Contact page before your package is dispatched.

What is your return and refund policy?

We offer a 14-day return and exchange policy from the date of item delivery, provided the item is in its original condition.

Are all products original and covered by warranty?

Yes, 100% of our products are original, quality-checked, and come with an official vendor warranty.

Are there any additional shipping fees?

Shipping fees are calculated transparently and shown on the Purchase Review page prior to finalizing your order.

What should I do if I receive a defective product?

Please contact our support team immediately via the Contact page. We will organize a free replacement within 24 to 48 hours.

How can I track my shipment status?

A tracking code and link will be emailed and texted to you as soon as your order leaves our warehouse.

Can I cancel my order before delivery?

Yes, you can cancel your order free of charge at any time before the shipping status updates to "Dispatched".
